Design isn’t about playing it safe or making things look pretty and polite—it’s about hitting people with a visual punch before they even read a single line of text. For me, it's this awesome puzzle where you take complete chaos—gritty analog textures, distorted type, and heavy contrasts—and lock it down inside a tight digital grid. Good design doesn’t just sit there blending into the background; it disrupts the room, scraps the boring templates everyone else is using, and builds a completely custom universe that people can't ignore.
